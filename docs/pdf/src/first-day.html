Your first day with Huddle

You joined a team that works with Claude Code and Huddle. Tasks arrive as Issues with their prompt ready; you start one with a command, answer the AI's questions and decide. This guide sets up your computer once, from zero to your first Issue.

The AI writes, tests and asks; you decide. Your answers to its questions are the work.

Rules

Five things that are not negotiable

  • Never push to the default branch, and never merge. Only the AI Manager merges.
  • Only the Issue's allowed files. If another one is needed, ask first.
  • No production and no secrets. If a permission or a key is missing, stop and tell the AI Manager.
  • Nothing is dropped silently. What is not done goes under «What was NOT done» with the reason.
  • Wait for the closing message. «✅ Issue #N finished» or «⛔ Issue #N stopped» tells you the session is over and what your next step is.
