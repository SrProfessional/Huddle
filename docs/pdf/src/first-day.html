Your first day with Huddle

You joined a team that works with Claude Code and Huddle. Tasks arrive as Issues with their prompt ready; you start one with a command, answer the AI's questions and decide. This guide sets up your computer once, from zero to your first Issue.

The AI writes, tests and asks; you decide. Your answers to its questions are the work.

Rules

Five things that are not negotiable

  • Never push to the default branch, and never merge. Only the Coach merges.
  • Only the Issue's allowed files. If another one is needed, ask first.
  • No production and no secrets. If a permission or a key is missing, stop and tell the Coach.
  • Nothing is dropped silently. What is not done goes under «What was NOT done» with the reason.
  • Several at once. /huddle-play alone works on several of your assigned Issues at once, as many as your capacity allows; huddle next shows which.
  • Wait for the closing message. «✅ Issue #N finished» or «⛔ Issue #N stopped» tells you the session is over and what your next step is. Then the session waits for the review in the background: it uses no Claude tokens and no GitHub Actions or CI minutes, so leave it open.

Every change to an Issue goes through a Huddle session, never by hand on the website. Still fine by hand: reading and commenting, the bug report form, and a tester's «✅ Tested in staging» comment. If someone breaks the rule, the next session that opens repairs the board by itself.
